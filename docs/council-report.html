Council report: Ammarc (formerly Armature)

Should we build Ammar's idea-to-image platform as specified, and how do we make it understand him? (Five advisors, anonymous peer review, chairman synthesis. 21 Oct 2026.)

Recommendation

Build it within the nephew's layout (elements, board, assistant), around a single loop: photo → mark or drop → one sentence → only that area changes → before/after → pitch slide. Three guarantees make it obey him literally:

  • Paste-back. The original pixels outside the marked area are restored byte for byte.
  • App-drawn dimensions. The app draws measurements and notes. The image model never draws them, and the slide is labelled "concept".
  • Placed elements. Dropped elements carry their hinge side and size, visible before rendering, so placement is literal.

First step: confirm Alba's photo policy (answered: allowed), then run a $5-capped live proof on about 10 of his real requests before he sees it. Pass bar: 8 of 10 literal edits.

Where the council agrees

  • The risk is literal obedience, not the UI.
  • Paste-back is the guarantee.
  • The app draws dimensions.
  • Structured fields (hinge, size, keep-list) beat free prose.
  • The pitch slide is the real deliverable.
  • Learned rules must be visible and editable.
  • Test the core interactions on his phone.

Where it clashes, and how it was resolved

  • Cut the library, chat and voice, or keep them? Keep them, as the nephew required. The library earns its place because dropped elements are placed exactly.
  • Auto-fix loop or not? A background check offers a one-tap "Fix it". It never spends automatically.
  • Mock-first or real-first? Build on mocks, with the eval harness ready. Then a $5 live proof run when the keys arrive.

Blind spots caught in peer review

  • Dropped elements can be composited deterministically, so the model only blends them in. Peer review rated this the strongest new idea.
  • Agree a pass/fail bar with Ammar (8 of 10), and watch him use it unassisted.
  • Keep the pitch honest: label it as a concept and put the original photo beside the render.
  • Ops: keys stay on a server, there is a daily image cap, and the nephew maintains it.
  • Paste-back needs colour matching. Strip EXIF/GPS from uploads. Test Gulf-Arabic and mixed-language dictation.

Peer review

It turned critique into a cheap test, named the deterministic feature, gave a cut list and a schedule, and priced real testing.

Biggest blind spot: Expansionist (5 of 5 reviewers)

It treated the edit engine as solved, added scope, and widened the confidentiality exposure.
